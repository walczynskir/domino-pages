    Wszystkie gry

    Computer
    • Beginner: picks a random legal move.
    • Intermediate: favors scoring moves but sometimes picks a weaker one.
    • Advanced: maximizes immediate points and favors higher-pip tiles when tied.
    • Expert: estimates the opponent’s hand from visible tiles, draws and passes, then checks a reply and a counter.
    • Grandmaster: samples more possible hands and searches deeper when no tiles can be drawn.
    Game rules

    Score for exposed ends totalling a multiple of five; by default only 5–5 scores on opening.

    In manual mode, undeclared move points are lost. By default three false declarations lose the game.
